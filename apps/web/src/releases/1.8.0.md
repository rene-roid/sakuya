# Sakuya 1.8.0

### New
- **Characters.** Sakuya can find the characters in your images and videos and group each one across your library. The **Characters** tab lists them: name a group and every picture it's in gets that character tag, accept a suggestion when most of a group's solo images already carry one character tag, merge two groups, or say "not this one" to a single face. The viewer outlines each character it found, and you can name a face right there. **This is off by default**: turn on **Character detection** in Settings → AI Tagging and it downloads its models (about 195 MB) once. It is slow on small CPUs, so it runs in the background after each scan.
- **Search by description.** Switch the search box to **Describe** and type what a picture shows, like "a witch flying on a broom". It combines with your library, type and tag filters. **Also off by default**: turn on **Search by description** in Settings → AI Tagging and it downloads its model (about 155 MB), then indexes your files and every new one after a scan.
- **Trash.** Deleting no longer removes files for good. Deleted media waits in the trash for 30 days, and restoring it brings back its tags, like and boards. Open it from Settings → Storage, or empty it there.
- **Database backups.** Sakuya backs up its database every day and keeps the newest seven. Change how many it keeps, run one now, or see where they're saved in Settings → Storage.
- **The desktop app updates itself.** It downloads a new release in the background, backs up the database, and then asks whether to restart now or updates on the next start. A small notice shows while it works. **Update Automatically** in the tray menu turns this off.
- **Tag merges and implications.** Merge an alias into the tag it means (`deku` into `midoriya_izuku`) and every item, search and future tag lands on the right one. Make a character imply its series and the series tag follows the character everywhere. Manage them from a tag's menu or Settings → AI Tagging.
- **Display names for tags.** Rename how a tag reads on screen (`Mira Lune` for `mira`) from the viewer or the tag sidebar. Search and links still use the real name.
- **Downloads keep their site's tags.** gallery-dl downloads now save the post's metadata, and a scan reads it into artist, series, character and general tags, with a **View source** link back to the post. Artist and series tags get their own groups.
- **Discover shelves.** Rows above the feed for what's new since your last visit, things you liked a while ago, and "Because you like" a tag, each with **See all**. The surprise slider becomes Familiar / Balanced / Adventurous.
- **Steer Discover.** The taste panel shows what the feed thinks you like. Boost a tag, see less of it, or mute it; mark a card **Not interested**, with Undo. **More like this** from a card or the viewer finds items that share its rarer tags, and with search by description on, ones that look alike.
- **Scrub videos from their card.** Move the pointer across a video's thumbnail to skim through it.
- **Search settings.** Every setting is on one page, grouped into sections, with a search box that finds a setting by what it does. Press `/` to jump to it.

### Improved
- **Videos are tagged from five frames** spread across them instead of one, so a video whose scenes change gets tags for all of them. Frames that look like one already scored are skipped, so a still video costs about the same as before; a video whose scenes keep changing takes about five times as long to tag.
- **Character tags have their own threshold**, 85% by default, next to the general one, because a wrong character name is worse than a missing one.
- **AI tags you remove stay removed.** A re-tag no longer puts back a tag you took off.
- **Discover feels less repetitive.** The feed spreads out items from one folder, items credited to the same tag, and near-identical shots. Scores count an item's five best tags rather than all of them, so heavily tagged items no longer win by volume, and likes and views from months ago slowly count for less.
- **Uploads and Duplicates have better homes.** Drop files onto a library, or use its **Upload** button; progress follows you around the app. Duplicates is its own page, reached from a library's menu or Settings → Media processing.
- **Settings → About shows how this copy updates**: the desktop app, Docker or a source checkout, each with its own steps.
- Implied tags are outlined in the viewer, so they read as derived.

### Fixed
- Flicking through the viewer no longer counts every item you passed as viewed. A view now counts once an item has been open for 2 seconds, so Discover stops treating skimmed items as seen.
- The phone dock fits all six labels, and Settings rows keep to one line on a phone.
